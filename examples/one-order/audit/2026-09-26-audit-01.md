# Audit 01: OneOrder, measured against its evidence

- Clip: `examples/one-order/clip.py`, scene `OneOrder`, as rendered in `examples/one-order/OneOrder.mp4`
  (1920x1080, 60 fps, 2346 frames, 39.10 s).
- Study directory: `examples/one-order/`
- The sentence the clip must land: "Jaeger sees three stories. The order id makes them one."
- Method: `vizln/anim/AUDITOR.md`. Measure, do not fix. Nothing outside `audit/` was edited. No network,
  no cluster. The mp4 was not re-rendered: every frame below comes from the final file.
- Every re-derivation here uses my own `jq` or Python over `evidence/`. `derive.py` was read (to name
  the key that produced a figure) but never run.

Scripts written for this pass, all in `audit/`: `rederive.py` (traces, hops, joins), `census.py`,
`clock_runs.py`, `probe_ink.py`, `probe_audit.py`, `probe_opacity.py`, `probe_beats.py`,
`sha256.expected`. Frames are in `audit/frames/`.

Ranking: section A holds findings that change what the clip says. B holds scope limits on figures
that are numerically right. C holds everything that agrees, and faults that do not change the claim.

## B. Scope limits on figures that are numerically right

### B1. "three" traces is a lower bound, and 11 of 18 answers were not kept

```sh
jq -r '.steps[] | "\(.n) \(.file // "not kept") \(.count)"' capture.json
```
Steps 3 to 13 have `"file": null`: the 21-service list and all ten linked-trace searches are counts
only. `capture.py:118` skips the 11 services already in the checkout trace, so a linked trace in
cart, email or any in-trace service could not be found. Each search had `limit=500` over 300 s. Only
direct FOLLOWS_FROM links to `a4013e83` count. "Jaeger sees three stories" is true of what was
searched. The 0 and 1 counts cannot be re-derived from disk.

### B2. "Cloud Logging: 3 lines, and none from checkout, frontend" measures textPayload only

`capture.json` step 18: `... AND textPayload:"7c8c01c4-ba22-11f1-903b-b295684fc89c"`. A jsonPayload
line would not match. The OpenSearch lines from checkout and frontend name the order only in
`attributes.demo.order.id`, not in the body. The absence is measured for one field, not for the store.
