## How people learn from a picture

- Gentner, D. (1983). Structure-Mapping: A Theoretical Framework for Analogy. *Cognitive
  Science*, 7(2), 155-170. The reason `PATTERNS.md` sorts by structure, not by topic.
- Bruner, J. S. (1966). *Toward a Theory of Instruction*. Harvard University Press. Enactive,
  then iconic, then symbolic. A clip is the iconic step.
